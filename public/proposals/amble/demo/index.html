Common questions

Do you cover my area?

We cover Crouch End (N8), Highgate (N6), Muswell Hill (N10), Alexandra Palace (N22), and surrounding streets. If you're not sure, WhatsApp us your postcode and we'll tell you straight.

How many dogs per group?

Four maximum. We match dogs by temperament and play style, not by who lives on the same street. If your dog doesn't suit groups, we offer solo walks too.

What if my dog is nervous or reactive?

No problem. George is IMDT qualified in canine body language, aggression, and rehabilitation. We do not throw dogs into situations they cannot handle. We build trust at their pace. Solo walks available if groups are not right for your dog.

Will it be the same person every time?

Yes. George walks your dog. George sits your dog. That's the whole model. No rotating staff. No strangers.

How do I get started?

Book a free meet & greet. We'll introduce your dog to George, talk through what you need, and do a trial walk. No commitment. No pressure. If it works, it works.
