Crouch End N8

Pub

The Boogaloo

Archway Road. Dogs welcome. Good Sunday roast. Water bowls at the bar.

Pub

The King's Head

Crouch End Broadway. Classic pub. Dog treats behind the counter if you ask.

Cafe

Character Coffee

Crouch End. Independent. Dog friendly inside and out. Good flat white.

Park

Parkland Walk

Four and a half miles of traffic-free path. Old railway line turned nature reserve. Crouch End to Finsbury Park.

Park

Highgate Wood

Ancient woodland. 70 acres. Ten minutes from Crouch End Broadway. Endless paths.

Park

Queen's Wood

Quieter than Highgate Wood. Dense. Atmospheric. Good for dogs who prefer fewer people.

We walk dogs in all three of these parks every day. Crouch End dog walking. £22/hr. Free meet and greet.

Highgate N6

Pub

The Flask

Highgate Village. Historic. Dogs welcome in the bar. Good after a walk on the Heath.

Pub

The Wrestlers

North Road. Traditional. Dog friendly. Thai food. Local institution.

Park

Hampstead Heath

800 acres. Parliament Hill views. Swimming ponds. Endless off-lead space. The best dog walk in North London.

Cafe

Kafenion

Highgate. Independent coffee. Dogs welcome. Good pastries.

Muswell Hill N10

Park

Alexandra Park

196 acres. Panoramic London views. Woodland and grassland. Palace grounds. Room to run.

Park

Coldfall Wood

Ancient woodland. Quiet. Local secret. Good for dogs who like sniffing more than socialising.

Pub

The Mossy Well

Muswell Hill Broadway. Wetherspoons but dog friendly. Water bowls. Cheap food.

Cafe

Lauderdale House Cafe

Waterlow Park. Dog friendly terrace. Good cake. Park views.

Alexandra Palace N22

Park

Alexandra Park

196 acres attached to the palace. Boating lake. Deer enclosure. Farmers market on Sundays. One of the best dog spots in North London.

Walk

Parkland Walk (northern section)

From Alexandra Palace down toward Highgate. Old railway path. Traffic free. Shaded in summer.

Pub

The Starting Gate

Station Road. Dog friendly. Near the Palace. Good after a walk.

Cafe

The Palace Cafe

Inside Alexandra Palace. Dogs welcome. Warming up after a winter walk.
